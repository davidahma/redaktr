# Evaluate the support-ticket workflow

This is a pilot protocol for a support team deciding whether to use Redaktr. The question is whether an engineer can share a useful image with fewer exposed details and an acceptable review time. The project has no field results to report here.

1. Recruit five Mac-based support engineers who already attach screenshots to tickets. Ask each to prepare three matched pairs of tasks based on the kinds of screens they actually share. Use invented or approved local examples. Keep customer material and policy files on the participant's Mac.
2. Before testing, have a second reviewer identify the sensitive regions and the details the recipient needs to diagnose each issue. Record the category and region without copying the sensitive value into the score sheet.
3. Alternate which workflow each engineer tries first. Use one image from each pair with the engineer's usual method and the other with Redaktr. Time import through final copy or save. Record the manual boxes and corrections required.
4. Have the reviewer inspect only the final images, without workflow labels. Record critical details still visible, unnecessary masks that remove diagnostic evidence, and whether the ticket remains useful. Count regions and images separately. An image with one critical miss counts as a failed share even if its other masks are correct.
5. After a week, ask which tool participants used the next time a comparable ticket came up. Report the number who had an opportunity and the number who chose Redaktr. No analytics integration or screenshot upload is needed.

Publish aggregate denominators, median task time for each workflow, missed critical regions by category, and the most common correction. Agree on an acceptable time with the team before the pilot. A missed critical region triggers a new synthetic case and another trial of that workflow. Use the comparison and the team's agreed criteria to decide whether to keep using Redaktr for this ticket type. Report this as a fifteen-pair pilot, with its sample size alongside every result.
